Privacy & Data

Titan Analyze is currently a local prototype.

Report data is saved in your browser. Audio is available during the current session but is not permanently stored. No information is sent to an AI service in this prototype.

AI Analysis Service

Connect Titan Analyze to a private server endpoint that accepts recorded audio and returns a structured analysis.

For security, the app does not contain an AI provider key. GitHub Pages cannot securely store one. Enter the URL of your private Titan Analyze endpoint below.
